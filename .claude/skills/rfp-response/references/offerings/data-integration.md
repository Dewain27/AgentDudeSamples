# Data Integration & Pipelines  (`data-integration`)

_Move data reliably between the systems that need it._

**Product line:** Data & Analytics  
**Scope:** design and delivery of reliable, monitored data pipelines that move and transform data between systems, eliminating manual exports and dual entry.  
**Best fit for:** healthcare, financial services, manufacturing, retail, education, government.  
**Indicative pricing:** Fixed price by workstream, invoiced on milestones · $160,000–$450,000 · 6 months is the typical full-scope engagement.

## Capabilities to describe in the proposed solution

- Automated extract, transform, and load (ETL/ELT) pipelines
- Scheduled and event-driven data movement
- Data validation and reconciliation
- Error handling, retries, and alerting
- Data lineage and processing audit trail
- Secure handling of sensitive fields

## Requirements this offering answers

If the RFP asks for these, this offering is the right match.

- Eliminate manual data exports and dual entry
- Move data automatically between systems on a schedule or event
- Validate and reconcile data in flight
- Alert on failures with clear diagnostics
- Maintain lineage and an audit trail
- Handle sensitive fields securely

## Integrations

Typical systems this connects to — name the buyer's actual systems when the RFP identifies them, and keep these as the fallback.

- the identity provider (SSO)

## Approved positioning language

Use these as the backbone of sections 3 and 4. Adapt the wording to the buyer's own terms; keep the substance.

**Understanding of needs:** Your teams manually export and re-key data between systems, and errors slip through unnoticed. You need automated, monitored pipelines you can trust.

**Proposed solution:** We will build automated ETL/ELT pipelines — scheduled or event-driven — that move and transform data between your systems with validation, reconciliation, lineage, and clear failure alerting, so manual exports and dual entry go away.
