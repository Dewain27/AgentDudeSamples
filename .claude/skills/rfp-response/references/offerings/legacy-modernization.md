# Legacy Application Modernization  (`legacy-modernization`)

_Retire risky legacy systems without a big-bang rewrite._

**Product line:** Custom Application Development  
**Scope:** incremental modernization of an aging, hard-to-maintain application onto a supported, cloud-native architecture with no loss of business function.  
**Best fit for:** government, financial services, healthcare, manufacturing, education.  
**Indicative pricing:** Fixed price per phase, invoiced on milestones · $400,000–$1,200,000 · 12 months is the typical full-scope engagement.

## Capabilities to describe in the proposed solution

- Assessment of the existing system and modernization roadmap
- Incremental re-platforming (strangler-fig approach)
- Data migration with validation and reconciliation
- Preservation and improvement of existing business rules
- Cloud-native, supportable target architecture
- Parallel-run and cutover planning

## Requirements this offering answers

If the RFP asks for these, this offering is the right match.

- Replace an unsupported or high-risk legacy application
- Preserve existing business rules and data
- Minimize disruption during transition
- Migrate historical data with validation
- Move to a supportable, cloud-hosted platform
- Reduce ongoing maintenance cost and risk

## Approved positioning language

Use these as the backbone of sections 3 and 4. Adapt the wording to the buyer's own terms; keep the substance.

**Understanding of needs:** You are carrying an aging system that is expensive and risky to maintain, and you need to modernize it without disrupting the business or losing years of data and hard-won business rules.

**Proposed solution:** We modernize incrementally using a strangler-fig approach: new, cloud-native components take over function piece by piece behind a stable interface, so the old system is retired gradually and safely. Data is migrated with validation and reconciliation, and every business rule is re-verified as it moves.
