# DevOps & Platform Automation  (`devops-automation`)

_Ship faster and sleep better with automated delivery._

**Product line:** Cloud & Platform Engineering  
**Scope:** establishment of CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure-as-code, and observability so software ships frequently, safely, and repeatably.  
**Best fit for:** financial services, healthcare, manufacturing, retail, government, education.  
**Indicative pricing:** Fixed price by workstream, invoiced on milestones · $150,000–$400,000 · 5 months is the typical full-scope engagement.

- CI/CD pipeline design and implementation
- Infrastructure-as-code for all environments
- Automated testing and quality gates
- Monitoring, logging, and alerting (observability)
- Secrets management and secure deployment
- Release and rollback automation
