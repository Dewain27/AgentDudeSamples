# Mobile Application Suite  (`mobile-app-suite`)

_Field-ready mobile apps that work even offline._

**Product line:** Custom Application Development  
**Scope:** design and delivery of native-quality mobile applications for frontline and field staff, with reliable offline operation and sync.  
**Best fit for:** manufacturing, government, retail, nonprofit.  
**Indicative pricing:** Fixed price by workstream, invoiced on milestones · $260,000–$600,000 · 8 months is the typical full-scope engagement.

## Capabilities to describe in the proposed solution

- Offline-capable data capture with automatic sync
- Task and work-order assignment and tracking
- Photo, signature, and barcode/QR capture
- GPS and location tagging of records
- Push notifications for new assignments
- Supervisor dashboard for field activity

## Requirements this offering answers

If the RFP asks for these, this offering is the right match.

- Mobile app for field or frontline staff
- Reliable operation with no or intermittent connectivity
- Capture of photos, signatures, and scanned codes
- Real-time visibility of field activity for supervisors
- Sync of field data into the core system of record
- Secure sign-in for staff devices

Use these as the backbone of sections 3 and 4. Adapt the wording to the buyer's own terms; keep the substance.

**Understanding of needs:** Your field teams need tools that work wherever they are — including with no signal — and that get clean data back into your core systems without manual re-entry.
