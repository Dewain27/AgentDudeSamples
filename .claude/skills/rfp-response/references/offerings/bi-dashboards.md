# Business Intelligence & Dashboards  (`bi-dashboards`)

_Self-service dashboards your business teams actually use._

**Product line:** Data & Analytics  
**Scope:** design and delivery of governed, self-service dashboards and reporting that give business users answers without waiting on IT.  
**Best fit for:** retail, financial services, manufacturing, healthcare, education, government, nonprofit.  
**Indicative pricing:** Fixed price by workstream, invoiced on milestones · $150,000–$400,000 · 5 months is the typical full-scope engagement.

## Capabilities to describe in the proposed solution

- Executive and operational dashboards
- Self-service reporting for business users
- Governed metric definitions everyone shares
- Scheduled and ad-hoc report export
- Drill-down from summary to detail
- Role-based access to sensitive views

## Requirements this offering answers

If the RFP asks for these, this offering is the right match.

- Let business users answer their own questions

- Enforce role-based access to sensitive data
