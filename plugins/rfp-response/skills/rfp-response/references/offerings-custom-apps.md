## Legacy Application Modernization — `legacy-modernization`

_Retire risky legacy systems without a big-bang rewrite._

**Scope:** incremental modernization of an aging, hard-to-maintain application onto a supported, cloud-native architecture with no loss of business function.  
**Best fit:** government, financial services, healthcare, manufacturing, education.  
**Compliance:** SOC 2, WCAG 2.1 AA.  
**Commercials:** Fixed price per phase, invoiced on milestones · $400,000–$1,200,000 · 12 months.

### Capabilities to describe in the proposed solution

- Assessment of the existing system and modernization roadmap
- Incremental re-platforming (strangler-fig approach)
- Data migration with validation and reconciliation
- Preservation and improvement of existing business rules
- Cloud-native, supportable target architecture
- Parallel-run and cutover planning

### Requirements this offering answers

- Replace an unsupported or high-risk legacy application
- Preserve existing business rules and data
- Minimize disruption during transition
- Migrate historical data with validation
- Move to a supportable, cloud-hosted platform
- Reduce ongoing maintenance cost and risk

### Integrations

Name the buyer's actual systems when the RFP identifies them; these are the fallback.

- the legacy database
- downstream systems that consume legacy data
- the identity provider (SSO)

### Differentiators

- Incremental strangler-fig approach avoids risky big-bang cutovers
- Business rules are re-verified, not blindly copied

### Success measures buyers care about

Mirror these in the executive summary when the RFP states similar goals — it shows you read their objectives.

- legacy system retired with no loss of function
- historical data migrated and reconciled
- measurable reduction in maintenance cost and risk

### Approved positioning language

**Understanding:** You are carrying an aging system that is expensive and risky to maintain, and you need to modernize it without disrupting the business or losing years of data and hard-won business rules.

**Solution:** We modernize incrementally using a strangler-fig approach: new, cloud-native components take over function piece by piece behind a stable interface, so the old system is retired gradually and safely. Data is migrated with validation and reconciliation, and every business rule is re-verified as it moves.
