## Enterprise Data Warehouse — `data-warehouse`

_One governed source of truth for your data._

**Scope:** consolidation of data from multiple source systems into a governed cloud data warehouse with a documented, trustworthy data model.  
**Best fit:** healthcare, financial services, manufacturing, retail, education, government.  
**Compliance:** SOC 2, data-governance and retention policies.  
**Commercials:** Fixed price by workstream, invoiced on milestones · $300,000–$900,000 · 9 months.

### Capabilities to describe in the proposed solution

- Automated data pipelines from all major source systems
- Governed, documented data model and definitions
- Role-based access to sensitive data
- Data-quality monitoring and alerting
- Historical and point-in-time reporting
- Foundation for BI, analytics, and AI

### Requirements this offering answers

- Consolidate data from many disconnected systems
- Establish a governed single source of truth
- Automate slow, manual reporting pulls
- Enforce role-based access to sensitive data
- Monitor and alert on data quality
- Support historical and point-in-time analysis

### Integrations

Name the buyer's actual systems when the RFP identifies them; these are the fallback.

- each core operational system
- the identity provider (SSO)
- the BI / dashboard tool

### Differentiators

- Governed, documented model — analysts trust the numbers
- Cloud-native warehouse scales without re-platforming

### Success measures buyers care about

Mirror these in the executive summary when the RFP states similar goals — it shows you read their objectives.

- at least eight source systems consolidated
- reporting time reduced from days to minutes
- a single, documented source of truth adopted across teams

### Approved positioning language

**Understanding:** Your data is spread across many systems, reporting is slow and manual, and teams argue about whose numbers are right. You need one governed source of truth.

**Solution:** We will build a cloud-native data warehouse fed by automated pipelines from your source systems, on a documented and governed data model with role-based access and data-quality monitoring — the trustworthy foundation for reporting, analytics, and AI.

## Data Integration & Pipelines — `data-integration`

_Move data reliably between the systems that need it._

**Scope:** design and delivery of reliable, monitored data pipelines that move and transform data between systems, eliminating manual exports and dual entry.  
**Best fit:** healthcare, financial services, manufacturing, retail, education, government.  
**Compliance:** SOC 2, data-governance and retention policies.  
**Commercials:** Fixed price by workstream, invoiced on milestones · $160,000–$450,000 · 6 months.

### Capabilities to describe in the proposed solution

- Automated extract, transform, and load (ETL/ELT) pipelines
- Scheduled and event-driven data movement
- Data validation and reconciliation
- Error handling, retries, and alerting
- Data lineage and processing audit trail
- Secure handling of sensitive fields

### Requirements this offering answers

- Eliminate manual data exports and dual entry
- Move data automatically between systems on a schedule or event
- Validate and reconcile data in flight
- Alert on failures with clear diagnostics
- Maintain lineage and an audit trail
- Handle sensitive fields securely

### Integrations

Name the buyer's actual systems when the RFP identifies them; these are the fallback.

- each source and destination system
- the identity provider (SSO)
- monitoring/alerting channels

### Differentiators

- Reconciliation built in — you know the data arrived intact
- Observable pipelines with lineage and clear failure alerts

### Success measures buyers care about

Mirror these in the executive summary when the RFP states similar goals — it shows you read their objectives.

- manual exports and dual entry eliminated
- data moved reliably with reconciliation
- failures surfaced and resolved via alerting

### Approved positioning language

**Understanding:** Your teams manually export and re-key data between systems, and errors slip through unnoticed. You need automated, monitored pipelines you can trust.

**Solution:** We will build automated ETL/ELT pipelines — scheduled or event-driven — that move and transform data between your systems with validation, reconciliation, lineage, and clear failure alerting, so manual exports and dual entry go away.
