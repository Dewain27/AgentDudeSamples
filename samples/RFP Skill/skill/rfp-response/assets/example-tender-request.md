## 1. Background

Cedar Valley Water District maintains 1,340 miles of distribution main, impounding
reservoirs, and 47 pump stations across a largely rural service area. Ninety-one
field technicians carry out scheduled inspections, meter work, and emergency
repairs.

Inspections are recorded on printed forms, returned to the district office at the
end of each shift, and re-keyed into our maintenance system by two administrative
staff. Backlogs of three to five days are routine. Cellular coverage across the
service area is patchy; approximately 30% of the district has no reliable signal.

## 2. Requirements

The application shall:

R1. Allow technicians to complete inspection and work-order forms on a
    district-issued mobile device.
R2. Function fully without network connectivity, synchronising automatically when
    a connection becomes available. This is a mandatory requirement.
R3. Capture photographs and attach them to the relevant asset record.
R4. Capture technician signatures for completed work.
R5. Read asset barcodes and QR tags.
R6. Record GPS coordinates against each inspection.
R7. Notify technicians of newly assigned work orders.
R8. Provide supervisors with a view of work in progress across the district.
R9. Integrate with the district's Cityworks maintenance management system.
R10. Authenticate technicians against the district's existing Microsoft accounts.
R11. Meet WCAG 2.1 AA.

## 3. Budget

The District has secured **$180,000** for this procurement. Tenders exceeding this
figure will not be considered. Tenderers are encouraged to identify any
functionality that cannot be delivered within this budget and to propose how it
might be addressed in a subsequent phase.

## 4. Required tender structure

Tenders **shall** be organised in the following order. Tenders not following this
structure may be set aside without evaluation.

- **Part A** — Tenderer details and authorised signature
- **Part B** — Compliance statement against requirements R1–R11, in a table,
  stating Fully Compliant / Partially Compliant / Not Compliant for each, with a
  brief note
- **Part C** — Technical solution
- **Part D** — Delivery programme and milestones
- **Part E** — Personnel
- **Part F** — Previous comparable work
- **Part G** — Pricing schedule
- **Part H** — Assumptions and exclusions

Tenders shall not exceed **20 pages** excluding appendices.
