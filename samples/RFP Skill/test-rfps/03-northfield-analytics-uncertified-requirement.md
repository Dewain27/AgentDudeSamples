## 1. About Northfield Regional Health System

Northfield Regional Health System operates four hospitals and twenty-two
outpatient clinics across the upper Midwest, employing approximately 6,800 staff
and serving roughly 410,000 patients annually.

## 2. Statement of need

Leadership currently receives operational and financial reporting through a
combination of manual spreadsheet extracts, vendor-supplied reports, and ad-hoc
queries run by our small analytics team. Producing the monthly executive packet
takes eleven business days. Different departments routinely report different
figures for the same measure — most visibly "patient encounters," for which
Finance, Operations, and Quality each maintain a separate definition.

We are seeking a vendor to help us establish trustworthy, timely reporting. We
have deliberately not prescribed a technical solution; we expect the vendor to
recommend an approach and explain the reasoning.

## 4. Requirements

### 4.1 Functional

4.1.1 Consolidate data from the source systems listed in Section 3.
4.1.2 Establish governed, documented definitions for core measures, with a
      process for resolving conflicting departmental definitions.
4.1.3 Provide executive dashboards covering financial, operational, and quality
      measures.
4.1.4 Allow department analysts to build their own reports without IT assistance.
4.1.5 Support drill-down from summary figures to underlying detail.
4.1.6 Support scheduled distribution of recurring reports.
4.1.7 Maintain historical data to permit period-over-period and point-in-time
      analysis.

### 4.2 Technical

4.2.1 Cloud-hosted; Northfield has an existing Azure tenancy and a stated
      preference for remaining within it.
4.2.2 Single sign-on through our existing Microsoft Entra ID.
4.2.3 Role-based access control, with row-level restriction of patient-identifying
      data.
4.2.4 Automated data-quality monitoring with alerting on pipeline failures.
4.2.5 Documented, versioned data model.

### 4.3 Security and compliance

4.3.1 All protected health information must be handled in accordance with HIPAA.
4.3.2 **The selected vendor must hold current HITRUST CSF certification.**
      Vendors shall state their certification status and, if certified, the
      version and expiration date.
4.3.3 Encryption of data in transit and at rest.
4.3.4 Annual independent security testing.
4.3.5 Audit logging of access to patient-identifying data, retained seven years.

## 5. Budget

Northfield has budgeted **$400,000 to $850,000** for initial implementation.
Ongoing hosting and licence costs should be estimated separately and are not
counted against this range.
