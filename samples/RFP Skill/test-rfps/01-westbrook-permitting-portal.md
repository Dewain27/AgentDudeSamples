## 1. Background

The City of Westbrook (population 96,000) seeks a vendor to deliver a
public-facing portal for building permits, business licenses, and
code-enforcement service requests.

Today residents and contractors must visit the Permit Center in person. Staff
track applications on paper and in a shared spreadsheet. Average building-permit
turnaround is 27 business days, and the Permit Center handles roughly 180 walk-in
visits and 400 phone enquiries a week during the spring construction season.

## 2. Requirements

1. Online application submission for permits and licenses, with document upload
2. Applicant status tracking and email/SMS notification of status changes
3. Configurable staff review and approval routing across departments
4. Online fee payment with receipts
5. Public dashboard showing application volumes and average processing times
6. Integration with our Esri ArcGIS parcel data
7. Integration with our Tyler Technologies financial system for fee posting
8. Single sign-on for staff via our existing Okta tenant
9. Section 508 and WCAG 2.1 AA conformance
10. Retention of application records per state schedule (7 years)

## 3. Budget

The City has budgeted **$450,000 to $900,000** for implementation. Ongoing
hosting and support should be quoted separately.
