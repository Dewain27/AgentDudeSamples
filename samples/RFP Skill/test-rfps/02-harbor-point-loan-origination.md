## 1. About Harbor Point Bank

Harbor Point Bank is a regional community bank operating 22 branches across
eastern Massachusetts, with approximately $3.1 billion in assets and 61,000
retail customers.

## 2. Statement of need

Our consumer and small-business lending process is largely manual. Applications
arrive by paper in branch or as PDF by email, are re-keyed into our core system,
and are routed for underwriting by internal email. Median time from application
to decision is 6.5 business days; our two largest regional competitors both
advertise same-day decisions.

We are seeking a vendor to deliver a digital origination and servicing platform.

## 4. Requirements

### 4.1 Functional

4.1.1 Online consumer loan application with document upload, on desktop and mobile.
4.1.2 Automated pre-qualification using configurable decisioning rules.
4.1.3 Customer self-service portal for application status, statements, and payments.
4.1.4 Staff underwriting workspace with queue management and audit history.
4.1.5 Configurable approval workflows by product and dollar threshold.
4.1.6 Electronic signature for closing documents.
4.1.7 Complete audit trail of every decision, override, and document version.

### 4.2 Technical

4.2.1 Cloud-hosted; the bank has no preference of provider.
4.2.2 Single sign-on for staff via Microsoft Entra ID.
4.2.3 Role-based access control with segregation of duties between origination and approval.
4.2.4 Integration with Fiserv Premier for account and balance data.
4.2.5 Integration with Experian for credit pulls, and DocuSign for signature.

### 4.3 Security and compliance

4.3.1 The solution must support our obligations under **GLBA**.
4.3.2 Encryption of data in transit and at rest.
4.3.3 Annual independent penetration testing.
4.3.4 **Vendors must describe their SOC 2 Type II status**, including the report
      period and any exceptions noted.
4.3.5 Audit logging retained for seven years.
