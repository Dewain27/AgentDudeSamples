## 1. About us

Open Horizons Foundation is a national education nonprofit supporting after-school
literacy programs in 40 states. Annual operating budget is approximately $28
million; we employ 130 staff and work with 2,400 volunteers.

## 2. What we need

Our visual identity has not changed since 2009 and no longer reflects the
organization we have become. We are seeking an agency partner to deliver a full
brand refresh and a national awareness campaign timed to our 25th anniversary in
September 2027.

## 3. Scope

S1. Brand strategy and positioning research, including donor and beneficiary
    interviews.
S2. New visual identity: logo, typography, colour system, photography direction.
S3. Brand guidelines document and asset library.
S4. Messaging framework and tone-of-voice guide.
S5. Campaign concept and creative development for the anniversary campaign.
S6. Media planning and buying across digital, out-of-home, and podcast.
S7. Campaign production: video, audio, print, and social assets.
S8. Rollout support across our website, collateral, and 60 chapter offices.

## 4. Note on our website

Our website will need to be re-skinned to the new identity. We already have a
web development vendor under contract who will do that work; the successful
agency will supply the design system and specifications, not build the site.

## 5. Budget

**$1,200,000 to $1,800,000** inclusive of media spend.
